So when kids start getting hyper they are unable to focus and they not only disturb their learning but the students around them as well. This shows that children could be at risk of developing a deadly disease that adults could have prevented. Also children may even die because heart disease is very dangerous. As you can see, chocolate milk is unhealthy because it has too much sugar and because it can cause diseases like type 2 diabetes and heart disease.</p>
